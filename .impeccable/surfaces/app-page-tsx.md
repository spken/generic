## Scope

Landing page (`app/page.tsx`), Persuade mode. Reusable template for appointment-based personal-care shops (barber, nail, brow, beauty studios); visitors are the shop's clients, mostly on phones. One action: Book an appointment (external booking URL from the content file).

## Surface strategy

- Audience job: decide in seconds whether this shop is open, good, affordable and near, then book.
- Proof available: only the shop's own facts from the content file (hours, services, prices, durations, team, address). No reviews, counts or awards are invented; gallery, portraits and the studio plate ship with clearly-marked sample stock photography (public/sample/PROVENANCE.md) until a shop supplies its own.
- Neutral by construction: no trade imagery in chrome.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is this week's page of the shop's appointment book. It refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed interior photo, serif name and gold "Book Now".

OWN-WORLD: Cool white ledger paper (not cream), graphite ink, pale blue ruling, one brand stamp ink carried by a single token. Condensed grotesk headings like printed ledger headers, tabular mono for times and prices, index tabs for navigation, rubber-stamp buttons, hatched graphite for closed time.

STORY: The visitor sees the shop's week ruled out with a live "now" line and whether it is open, reads the services as a priced ledger where duration is drawn as time cells, meets the team as columns of the book, finds the address and hours, and stamps Book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ledger header strip with wordmark, today's date, index tabs and a stamp Book button. Left: the shop name and one-line promise at display scale, open-status line, primary stamp Book. Right (below on phones): the week grid, Mon to Sun, opening hours ruled, today's column inked, the now line moving in real time.

FORM: Appointment Book, position 1 on my ordered list (chosen as Impeccable's pick over the roll), seed key 79712e07. Signature interaction: the live now-line on the week grid plus the stamp press on Book; motion grammar is ink being laid down (rules draw, stamps press), never floaty fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
